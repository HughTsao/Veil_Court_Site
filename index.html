Veilcourt 幕後王廷

A game of veiled schemes for 3–5 houses.

3–5 個家族的幕後權謀遊戲。

Frequently asked questions

How do I learn to play?

On the main menu, tap How to play for 11 short animated lessons and a card guide. You can practise every mini-game from Mini-games.

My friend can't find my nearby game

  • Both iPhones need Wi-Fi and Bluetooth turned on (you don't need to be on the same Wi-Fi network).
  • Both need the same version of Veilcourt.
  • Allow Local Network: Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network → Veilcourt.
  • Keep the host's lobby screen open. Scanning the host's QR code also works.

Why does Veilcourt ask for Local Network access?

To find and connect to friends' iPhones nearby for multiplayer. Game data only travels between the phones in your game.

I got disconnected in the middle of a game

Open Veilcourt again: it reconnects by itself and you keep your seat. If you're gone for a while, a bot plays for you until you're back.

Can I turn the mini-games off?

Yes. In the game setup (or Settings), choose Mini-games: Off, Duels (attacks only) or All.

Does Veilcourt collect my data?

No. There are no accounts, ads or analytics. See the Privacy Policy.

常見問題

要怎麼學會玩？

在主選單點 遊戲教學，有 11 段簡短的動畫教學和卡牌圖鑑。每個小遊戲都可以在 小遊戲 裡練習。

朋友找不到我建立的附近房間

  • 兩支 iPhone 都要開啟 Wi-Fi 與藍牙（不需要連上同一個 Wi-Fi 網路）。
  • 兩邊的幕後王廷要是相同版本。
  • 允許區域網路：設定 → 隱私權與安全性 → 區域網路 → 幕後王廷。
  • 主持人的房間畫面要保持開啟；掃描主持人的 QR 碼也可以加入。

為什麼幕後王廷要使用區域網路？

為了尋找並連線到附近朋友的 iPhone 進行多人遊戲。遊戲資料只會在同一局的手機之間傳送。

遊戲進行到一半斷線了

重新打開幕後王廷，它會自動重新連線，你的座位也會保留。如果離開較久，會由電腦暫時代打，直到你回來。

可以關掉小遊戲嗎？

可以。在開局畫面（或設定）選擇 小遊戲：關閉、對決（只有攻擊時）或 全部。

幕後王廷會收集我的資料嗎？

不會。沒有帳號、廣告或分析工具。詳見隱私權政策。
