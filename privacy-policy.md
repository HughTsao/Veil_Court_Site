# Veilcourt — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Veilcourt does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- **No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.** The app has no servers and sends nothing to the developer or to third parties.
- **Settings stay on your device.** Your language, sound and game preferences, and the name you type for nearby games, are saved only on your iPhone.
- **Nearby multiplayer stays local.** When you host or join a nearby game, the phones talk to each other directly over your local network or peer-to-peer Wi-Fi. The only things exchanged are the game itself and the player names you chose, and only with the phones in that game. This is why iOS asks for Local Network access.
- **Children.** Because no data is collected, none is collected from children.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
